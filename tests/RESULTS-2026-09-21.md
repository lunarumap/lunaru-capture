# TEST 0.16 — camera axis, reticle and recording probe

Baseline: published TEST 0.15 (`9fc2689`). Only `abc/` and test documentation changed. The original `base-013.js`, root Capture and other LUNARU modules are unchanged. IndexedDB name/schema retained; no deletion or rewriting of saved media.

## Changes

- Rear optical axis from all three W3C device-orientation angles; fixes ignored gamma and negative-beta branch. Relative sensor heading is not surveyed azimuth.
- Fixed crosshair, moving small target and dwell progress; compact metadata, no GPS prompt over the target and no indoor GPS request.
- Existing populated methods with a base angle retain legacy orientation; test the new model in a new object. Empty legacy methods reset their anchor.
- Native photo requests have bounded timeouts and explicit fallback confirmation. A failed maximum-resolution request may retry once with camera-selected settings; no silent downsize of originals.
- A 1.8-second recording probe is decoded completely and measures encoded frame rate. Below 12 fps is rejected before the pass; smaller modes are tried down to 854px/15fps without switching device or zoom. No promise of sustained performance after heating.
- Second automatic frame at the same pole requires leaving the pole first. Sphere coverage still explicitly unvalidated.

## Executed

`node tests/capture-abc.mjs` passed in Chromium 153.0.8010.0 with a simulated rear camera (640×480) and real browser MediaRecorder. Requires Playwright and fflate; executable and module paths can be supplied by CHROMIUM_PATH, PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE, FFLATE_MODULE.

Passed: 32 A frames, 40 B frames, retake, video pause/resume, all three ZIPs and original dimensions, exported video decoding, quota failure preservation, native photo dimensions and explicit fallback, permission in click stack, jitter/fast rotation/north wrap, recording startup errors, committed-chunk recovery after reload, second-tab lock, in-app playback. Added assertions for optical-axis invariance under Euler branch changes, zenith, missing sensor values, centered unobscured reticle at width 375 and heights 851/660/595, and a real 3fps probe measured below 6fps.

## Limits and handoff

No physical iPhone/Redmi 9/Redmi A3X test of 0.16 has been performed here. No stitching, full-sphere certification, or PWA-install test. A short probe cannot guarantee long-run thermal/encoder performance. Sensor drift and camera translation remain possible.

Open the existing HTTPS `/abc/?v=0.16.0` address, confirm TEST 0.16, create a new object, take 2–3 frames and a 10-second video, stop, reload and play/download. Do not clear site storage. Existing datasets remain available.

`tests/live-smoke.mjs` compares deployed assets byte-for-byte, records a simulated-camera video through HTTPS, reloads and plays it in isolated browser storage. It never modifies real user datasets.
